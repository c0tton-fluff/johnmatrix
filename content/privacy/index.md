Short version: this site collects almost nothing, and there is nothing to opt out of.

## What this site does not do

- No cookies. Not one.
- No accounts, no logins, no forms - there is nothing to breach.
- No tracking pixels, no fingerprinting, no ads, no third-party fonts or media.
- No email list, so no email addresses are stored.

## Analytics

Visitor analytics run on [Plausible](https://plausible.io/), a privacy-friendly service that collects no personal data and uses no cookies: page views, referrer, and coarse device/browser data, aggregated. Nothing identifies you individually, and nothing follows you across sites.

## Hosting

The site is static files served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard request data (IP address, user agent) under its own privacy statement - that is their infrastructure, not my code.
